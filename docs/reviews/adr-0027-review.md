# ADR-0027 review record

Reviews of [ADR-0027](../adr/0027-one-wallet-model.md): removing account-bound notes, R0 V6 and key custody. Each round ran independent read-only reviewers, and a skeptic tried to refute every finding before it counted. Every confirmed item below is applied in ADR-0027 revision 3. Full reviewer outputs are kept in the session's workflow journals.

## Round 1: four-lens review of revision 1 (2026-09-26)

Lenses: protocol and soundness, key custody and web security, completeness against the code, and consistency and accuracy. 54 findings: 47 confirmed and 7 rejected.

It covers the protocol removal and most of the earlier parity findings: shadow accounts, key rotation, the per-spend prompt, the per-account cap, submission and RPC separation, the backup check, the vault, and the open-note handle. **Consistency.** ADR-0027's technical core checks out against the repository. R0 V6 = V5 minus the mode bit, WAUTH and `account`. `TMR0_BIND_V5` never included the account (spend5.cairo:32-52), and the adapter binds the statement account, class and tag (adapter.cairo:69-73).

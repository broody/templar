Templar wallet signing test

P0b spike, throwaway page. Starknet Sepolia. Nothing is sent anywhere except to your wallet.

This page only asks your wallet to sign two test messages, twice each.
  • You will see one connect request and then four "sign message" prompts (Templar domain). It never asks for a transaction, a network switch, or any other permission. If your wallet shows anything else, reject it.
  • The messages are test values: a made-up pool address and a withdrawal of 0. No contract accepts them.
  • The first message uses the spike-only purpose TEMPLAR_SIGTEST_V0, so its signature is not a Templar secret.
  • Signatures stay on this page until you press Copy JSON.
